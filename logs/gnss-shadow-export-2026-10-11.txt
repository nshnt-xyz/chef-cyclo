Diagnostic method
-----------------
No image rebuild, daemon replacement, reboot, restore or automatic
persistence was needed. The installed RMTFS binary exactly matched the
local non-PIE ELF with retained symbols, SHA-256:
  0a417d2feb730b7a9f2e42e58bac12c49ef13f1c8fe2aa6598dfa705cb01ac78
A one-off static AArch64 helper imported the matching storage.c private
structures. Reviewed ELF symbols: rmtfds at 0x418970 (400 bytes),
storage_read_only at 0x418960. These addresses are valid only for this ELF.

The helper requires the expected executable path, a single thread and
RAM-shadow mode. PTRACE_SEIZE/INTERRUPT briefly stops the daemon, then
PTRACE_GETREGSET verifies x8=pselect6 and PC at the verified syscall/return
boundary (0x40bddc or 0x40bde0). The single-threaded request loop is not
updating shadow buffers at that boundary. /proc/PID/mem is opened O_RDONLY.
Copies are bounded to 16 MiB per shadow and 64 MiB total, and only the
four expected image names are accepted. RMTFS is detached/resumed before
snapshot payloads are written to disk. New directories and exclusive
0600 files prevent overwriting previous exports; COMPLETE is written
last after file/manifest synchronization.

The first attempt checked /proc/PID/syscall after interrupt; that reports
-1 for the interrupted syscall, so it safely refused export, detached,
and left an empty directory. The corrected helper verifies registers.
The empty directory was removed. A deliberate existing-directory retry
also refused before attaching, leaving TracerPid=0 and the daemon alive.
No EXITKILL option is used; tracer termination releases the tracee. The
copy phase has a 10-second watchdog. This is a diagnostic for the exact
verified build, not a general production snapshot interface.

Verification and limits
-----------------------
All four original backing-partition SHA-256 hashes stayed unchanged from
before the session to after release. Physical write-sector counters for
modemst1, modemst2, fsc, fsg_a, fsg_b and persist stayed zero in every
sample. All sixteen image hashes in the host's four extracted sets match
their device copies. Every successful export left TracerPid=0; RMTFS
continued serving the same boot, and GPS acquired a fix while using it.
The helper compiled with -Wall -Wextra -Werror.

Real RAM-content changes are now established, but changes spread across
every sector do not identify particular GNSS files or explain the image
encoding. No EFS decoder or filename attribution was attempted. These
snapshots are evidence for further analysis, not an approved restore
format; no cross-boot restore or GPS-startup benefit has been tested.
The separate TFTP in-process RAM filesystem was not exported.

Final state: GPS OFF with no leases/grace/failures/leaked clients, original
partitions unchanged, support daemons resident. Images persist in our own
/data and on the host. The temporary exporter remains under /run only;
the installed system binaries and boot image were not changed.

Offline format triage after export
---------------------------------
All eight modemst source/shadow images have byte entropy about
7.9999 bits/byte and no readable occurrences of the tested GNSS/orbit
terms. About 99.6 percent of bytes differ when an image is rewritten.
This is consistent with an encrypted or otherwise encoded full-image
representation, but does not prove encryption or identify the format.
file(1)'s Sony PlayStation classification of modem_fs1 is a false magic
match, not evidence of its real filesystem format. modem_fsg is recognized
as an ext2 filesystem with label FSG and remains byte-identical.
These raw byte differences cannot establish which logical settings,
last-position data, orbital assistance or housekeeping fields changed.
No claim that all logical contents changed follows from the byte counts.
