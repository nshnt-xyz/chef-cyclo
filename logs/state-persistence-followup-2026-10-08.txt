2. Implementation (reviewed by persist_review: review 1 on 9778f322,
review 2 on b5b28368, plus nits; final diff staged, hash in the commit
request)

powerd (tools/powerd.c): with -l, rows go to /run/power/log.pending and
reach LOGDIR/log.csv in batches every -F SECS (default 600; the
deadline is in the poll timeout); urgent rows flush at once (start row
with any leftover pending rows, plug/unplug, status, alert, throttle
level, shutdown row). Rotation and header-mismatch rotation inside a
batch, split at row boundaries; a failed or short write is truncated
back and the rows stay pending (logged once); pending bounded at
256 KiB (oldest rows dropped, one log line per episode); a torn pending
tail is dropped, never flushed; a short append to log.pending is
truncated back. Durable flushes fsync log.csv and LOGDIR once after the
appends: the shutdown row before the shutdown hook, the SIGTERM exit
flush, and the new one-shot `powerd -d RUNDIR -l LOGDIR [-L] flush`.
Before powerd's last-resort reboot(RB_POWER_OFF) a failed durable flush
is retried once. Without -l nothing changes.
chef-state shutdown: TERMs the timekeeper's whole process group (pgrp
== PID checked; else its descendants by parent PID), waits at most
about 1 s for the save lock (flock -n loop; BusyBox flock has no -w),
chronyc 1 s and rtc-edge 1.5 s in the shutdown save, the chronyc and
rtc-edge calls do not pass the lock fd to BusyBox timeout's watchdog
(8>&-), stop wait at least 0.3 s; then, only when the powerd seen at
entry is confirmed gone and no powerd is alive, appends rows a stopped
powerd left in /run/power/log.pending with `powerd ... flush`
(power_flush_rc in the summary line). Device check: timekeeper pgrp ==
PID on the installed pair (1058) and on the RAM images (904, 895).

Host: make -C tools test rc 0 on the final tree (host-tools-test4.txt):
test-powerd 226/226, test_powerd.sh 58/58 (strace: no fsync before TERM,
exactly 2 on TERM), test_chef_state.py 104 OK under bash and BusyBox
ash. Mutations checked by both of us are caught (see the review

4. What can still lose pending power rows (documented in
docs/features/battery-and-charging.md and storage.md)

Lossless: every orderly shutdown through init's ::shutdown lines
(reboot/poweroff from a shell, buttond, powerd's own poweroff,
::restart), and powerd's own poweroff even through its last-resort
reboot(RB_POWER_OFF) (rows fsynced before any shutdown command; a
failed flush retried once before the syscall). Up to one interval of
ordinary rows can be lost on: PMIC hardware reset (Power about 8.7 s),
sudden power loss, kernel panic or watchdog reset, reboot -f /
poweroff -f, the outer 5 s hook bound killing chef-state shutdown,
buttond's last-resort reboot(RB_POWER_OFF), or /data failing.
