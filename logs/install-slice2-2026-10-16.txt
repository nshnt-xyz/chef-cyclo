5. Found while clearing the install (not blockers)
IC-1: ::shutdown runs chef-state shutdown (sensors-up, powerd, the timekeeper, chronyd; up to 1.8 s) and then chef-storage shutdown (/data read-only); chef-settings,
blesensd and gps-manager are not stopped there, so their exit saves (the settings debounce and flush of up to 2 s, blesensd's 3 s, gps-manager's up to 3 s) run
against a read-only /data: a settings change, GPS switch or BLE record change made in the last 2 to 3 s before a reboot or power-off is lost (the writes are
atomic and sealed, so no file is corrupted; a writer holding settings.tmp at the remount could in theory make the read-only remount fail, milliseconds wide).
