Conclusions:
- Part D works end to end: learn, save, reboot, seed, applied at once.
  Keep it.
- Learning still needs slow varied holds and can take more than one
  round (here one round of ~2.5 min was not enough, the second flipped
  after ~84 s). With a saved bias this is needed once per hard-iron
  change, not once per boot.
- The stale-bias risk from the 2026-10-08 review (item 2) remains only
  for a changed hard iron (new mount, a magnet nearby): the seeded bias is
  then applied and reported as calibrated until the ADSP re-learns.
  SENSORS_FRESH=1 skips the overlay. No change made for that.
- The USB cable gets in the way of the holds; the guide runs on the phone
  on its own, so unplugging after the start works. Wireless control
  (SSH with key login) would be a separate task.
