Review corrections: strict RTC parsing/checked arithmetic and canonical
shell integers; hard 16 MiB crash cap and verified saved-copy dedup; narrow
mag group validation, atomic overlay and one/minute changed-write saves,
with deferred/failed saves retried even without another DSP write and final
exit snapshot; explicit early powerd TERM overlapping the RTC save;
PID/start-time-aware completion (Z/X exited, unreadable/malformed state
conservative); truthful time/mag/sync/stop outcomes. Normal shutdown targets
about 2 s with a shared approximate 1.8 s stop budget, not a hard wall bound.
Blocking failure paths retain the tested outer 5 s TERM + 1 s KILL hook cap;
this timeout targets the hook PID, not all descendants. Process exit does
not prove a drift write succeeded; drift readback is separate evidence.
Power rows are not fsynced individually; commit=5 is not a dirty-data
writeback deadline. Recent rows can be lost on abrupt power loss.
RTC time remains unverified until sync; a battery-reset counter can catch
up with the saved counter, so not every battery reset can be detected.
RMTFS fflush(stdout) is diagnostic only, with no payload logging.
