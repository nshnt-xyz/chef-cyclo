# Intermittent shutdown hang (2026-10-05)
# Raw evidence: ~/chef-cyclo-evidence/shutdown-hang-20261005/ (local). No secrets, no positions, no MACs, no serial.

## Symptom (boot compression run, morning)
3 of 15 ordinary reboots came back after 32-49 s (normal ~21 s) with androidboot.bootreason=kernel_panic,
powerup_reason 0x00020000, PON "Hard Reset and 'warm' boot"; ping stopped ~0.1-0.3 s after the last shutdown line.

## Tracing (installed 5c509eb2 + 9c47c6b8, kernel #21)
t01-t15 mirror tracer (t01 at 72 s, others 36.5-39.5 s uptime): 15/15 clean.
t16-t32 plain init reboots (t16 81 s, others 36.2-36.7 s): 17/17 clean (t16/t17 with the v1 monitor).
Seen at init's SIGTERM on every shutdown, harmless: gps-up closes /dev/subsys_modem within ~6 ms (modem stop takes the
WLAN firmware PD down while wpa_supplicant disconnects; "target access is not allowed" on WLAN CE registers); fblog's
fb release sends the bl_shutdown_dcs backlight command after the panel is off (DSI cmd DMA timeout every time,
clk_branch_wait WARN "dsi byte clk stuck off" in 2 of 15). pstore keeps no pmsg over a normal reboot.
0/32 vs 3/15 (one-sided Fisher 455/16215 = 0.028). Port type then APSD=CDP (battery ~40 C), now SDP: false lead.

## Located
t33: plain reboot at 200.7 s uptime, SDP: bootreason=kernel_panic, warm PON, ping gap 32.9 s. pstore on that boot:
  204.18   init's SIGTERM (gps-up modem stop, wlan disconnect, fblog exit)
  204.2779 Unable to handle kernel NULL pointer dereference at virtual address 00000040, CPU6 swapper, softirq
           PC _raw_spin_lock_irqsave+0x1c, LR hci_ibs_wake_retrans_timeout+0x28, call_timer_fn/run_timer_softirq
  204.2808 Kernel panic - not syncing: Fatal exception in interrupt
  210.4876 Going down for restart now; 210.4890 Causing a watchdog bite!
Cause: hci_qca qca_close(): del_timer() (not sync) x2, then destroy_workqueue() drains qca_wq_awake_device(), which
re-arms wake_retrans_timer (+100 ms); then hu->priv = NULL, kfree(qca), kfree(hu); the timer fires on freed data.
btattach is closed by init's SIGTERM at every shutdown. Second gap: the drained work schedules hu->write_work after
hci_uart_tty_close() cancelled it.

## Reproducer (no reboot of its own; /data remounted ro and abslot-marked boot required before any try)
Old kernel #21: both 0/10, traffic 0/10 (0/2/5/10 ms), bt 0/10, storage 0/20 (bluetoothd always left hci0 DOWN
within 2 s of TERM), up 0/10 (hci0 UP RUNNING, IBS TX asleep at the kill). 0/60: not reproducible on demand.
Fixed kernel #22 (fastboot boot of boot-ram 42f362b0, no flash): up/storage/both/traffic 40/40 survived, BT
re-attached every time, 0 WARNING/BUG/Spurrious/tx timeout/sleep-in-atomic/oops lines, btmgmt find works.
This is a functional check of the new teardown, not proof of the fix.

## Fix
kernel commit 8ef120353 "Bluetooth: hci_qca: stop timers and drain work before freeing qca"
(diff sha256 3075a1c476641ceb392702946318b10dc935b5c7ad150e5a71a459f11029913a): qca_close del_timer_sync x2,
flush_workqueue, del_timer_sync x2, destroy_workqueue, then skb_queue_purge; hci_uart_tty_close cancel_work_sync
(write_work) again after proto->close, outside proto_lock.
