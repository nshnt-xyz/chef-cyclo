# Battery and charging

[Feature index](README.md) · [Build instructions](../building.md) · [Stock research and implementation record](../research/battery-and-charging.md)

## Current behavior

Charging is done by the kernel, the same way stock Android does it: `qpnp-smb2` (PM660 charger: source detection, input and charge current limits, termination), the Motorola `mmi` heartbeat inside `smb-lib.c` (battery-temperature zones, step charging) and `qpnp-fg-gen3` (fuel gauge, battery profile picked by the battery serial). No userspace helper is needed for any of that.

`tools/powerd/` adds the small policy layer stock userspace puts on top. It runs as the supervised OpenRC service `powerd` ([services](services.md)), as user `powerd` with CAP_KILL for `poweroff` and the `vibrator` group ([users and permissions](users-and-permissions.md)); `system_temp_level` is root:powerd 0664 from the service's `start_pre`:

- **Low battery** (a clone of Android `BatteryService`). With no power attached, it warns once at 15 % and again at 5 % (critical, plus a 600 ms vibrator pulse), as `powerd` log lines ([logging](logging.md)). Both warnings re-arm when power is attached or the capacity is back at 20 %.
- **Clean shutdown** when unpowered, `battery/present == 1`, `bms/soc_reporting_ready == 1` and either the capacity is 0 % or `voltage_now` is below 3300 mV with the capacity at or below 5 %. Also when the battery is **above 68.0 °C**, whether or not a charger is attached: this is the only shutdown allowed while powered. Every condition must hold on 3 samples at least 5 s apart. An unreadable or `-22` value counts as unknown and restarts the count. The shutdown is `sync` then busybox `poweroff`, the same orderly path as `buttond`.
- **"Unpowered"** means `battery/status` reads exactly `Discharging` **and** `usb/online`, `pc_port/online` and `dc/online` each read 0 or their supply does not exist. An SDP (PC USB 2) port reads `usb/online = 0` and reports the input on `pc_port/online`, and an attached charger that is not charging reads `Not charging`, so neither counts as unplugged. Anything unreadable counts as powered. The sign of the current is never used.
- **Charge thermal throttle** (a clone of thermal-engine's `SS-BATT-BATT` rule). While a supply is online it checks every 5 s: battery ≥ 44.0 °C raises `battery/system_temp_level` by one step (up to level 7), ≤ 42.0 °C lowers it. The level indexes the DT `qcom,thermal-mitigation` FCC table (3000, 2500, 2000, 1500, 1200, 900, 700, 300 mA). It is written back to 0 at start, on unplug and on a clean exit.
- **Logging** (a clone of `batt_health`): one CSV row per uevent and poll. When `/data` is the mounted [persistent layout](storage.md#persistent-state) (`chef-state ours`), the service starts it as `powerd -l /data/v1/power -L 4194304` (its `start_pre` picks the arguments): the log survives reboots, bounded at 4 MiB with one rotation to `log.csv.1` (retention time depends on the uevent rate). Otherwise (RAM-only boot) it is `/run/chef/power/log.csv` ([runtime files](runtime-files.md)), 1 MiB plus one rotation, as before. On `/data` the rows are batched (since 2026-10-08): each row first goes to `/run/chef/power/log.pending` (tmpfs), and the pending rows are appended to `log.csv` in one write at least every 10 minutes (`-F SECS`, default 600). Rows that matter after a crash or power loss are flushed at once: the `start` row (together with any rows a previous powerd left in `log.pending` during the same boot), plug/unplug, a battery status change, a warn/critical/confirming/shutdown alert change and a throttle level change. Every orderly shutdown keeps the pending rows: busybox init runs `chef-state shutdown` as its first `::shutdown` line (also on the `::restart` path) whether the shutdown came from `reboot` or `poweroff` in a shell, a buttond long press or powerd itself, and the return to fastboot through `chef-reboot bootloader` (`scripts/phone-boot.sh` and the fastboot test and install workflow, since 2026-10-10; [storage](storage.md), [live check](../../logs/reboot-bootloader-2026-10-10.txt)) runs it with init paused before `chef-storage shutdown` and `btprobe restart bootloader`. Either way its SIGTERM makes powerd append the pending rows and `fsync` `log.csv` and the log directory before it exits, while `/data` is still writable. If powerd was not running (a crash or a respawn gap), `chef-state shutdown` appends what it left with `powerd -d /run/chef/power -l /data/v1/power -L 4194304 flush` (the same rules, fsynced), but only once no powerd is alive; while one might still write, it leaves the rows and says so in kmsg. powerd's own low-battery or over-temperature poweroff is lossless: the shutdown row and every pending row before it are appended and fsynced before any shutdown command runs, and if that flush failed, powerd tries once more before its last-resort `reboot(RB_POWER_OFF)` (used only when init refuses SIGUSR2). Pending rows (up to one interval of ordinary rows) are lost only when the shutdown skips that: the PMIC hardware reset (Power held about 8.7 s) or sudden power loss, a kernel panic or watchdog reset, `reboot -f`/`poweroff -f` (they bypass init's `::shutdown` lines), the outer 5 s bound killing a blocked `chef-state shutdown`, buttond's last-resort `reboot(RB_POWER_OFF)` when init refuses SIGUSR2, a bare raw reboot to the bootloader (`btprobe restart bootloader` typed directly, or `scripts/phone-boot.sh`'s fallback on an image built before `chef-reboot`: only `chef-storage shutdown --pause-init` runs first, and it stops only processes holding `/data` open, which powerd does not between flushes), or a flush that fails because `/data` is read-only or failing. Before batching, the same cases lost only the rows still in the writeback delay. Appending each row on its own had cost about 12 to 15 KiB of eMMC writes per 175-byte row (an ext4 journal commit each, about 90 % of all userdata writes when idle; [measurement](../../logs/state-persistence-followup-2026-10-08.txt)). A failed flush (read-only remount, full disk) keeps the rows in `log.pending`, is logged once and retried at the next important row or interval; a short write is truncated back so no partial row is left or repeated. `log.pending` is bounded at 256 KiB: while `log.csv` keeps failing, its oldest rows are dropped with one log line. A torn last row in `log.pending` (powerd killed mid-append) is dropped, never flushed. On a RAM-only boot (`/run/chef/power/log.csv`) rows are still appended one by one, with no `fsync`; a failed append is logged once and retried on the next row. `/run/chef/power/state` (current values as `key=value` lines, rewritten atomically) and `shutdown-pending` always stay on `/run`.
- **Log format:** a header line, then `uptime,utc,reason,status,source,usb_type,capacity,voltage_mv,current_ma,temp_c,health,charge_type,ocv_mv,charge_full_uah,charge_counter_uah,cycle_count,soc_ready,usb_current_max_ma,usb_input_ma,usb_voltage_mv,input_settled_ma,profile_fcc_ma,typec_mode,pc_port_online,dc_online,system_temp_level,throttle_level,alert,boot_id`. `reason` is `start`, `uevent`, `poll`, `tick` or `signal`. `boot_id` (the kernel's `/proc/sys/kernel/random/boot_id`) is filled on the `start` row only, so boots can be told apart in a log that spans several; it is empty on every other row. `utc` is 1970 until the clock is set (by `chef-state` from the saved RTC offset, or by chrony). A file whose header differs (an older format) is rotated to `log.csv.1` before the first new row.

Nothing else is written: no input/charge current overrides, no `input_suspend`, ship mode or demo mode, and nothing in persist or EFS. The kernel's own charging stays in charge. The charging LED stays off, as on stock.

### The sign of `current_now`, and the battery's charge attributes

Since kernel build #24 (2026-10-24, `qpnp-smb2.c`) the two supplies report the battery current with opposite signs:

| File | Sign | Read by |
|---|---|---|
| `battery/current_now` | the power_supply ABI: positive while charging, negative while discharging (µA) | UPower and any standard tool; UPower takes a negative current as discharging, whatever `status` says |
| `bms/current_now` | the fuel gauge's own (Qualcomm) sign: positive while discharging, negative while charging | powerd (`current_ma`, + = discharging, the same meaning as before #24) and every in-kernel user (the `mmi` heartbeat and `smb-lib` read the gauge through smblib or `bms`, so charging itself is unchanged) |

Before #24 `battery/current_now` had the Qualcomm sign too. The `battery` supply also gained, from `bms`: `charge_full`, `charge_full_design` and `cycle_count` as `bms` has them, and `charge_now`, which is `bms/charge_counter` (µAh; `bms/charge_now` itself reads 0). With them UPower has an energy and a time estimate. Nothing else in the kernel changed.

On the host PC's USB port the input current collapses to about 145 mA ([AICL](#live-verification-2026-09-26-qpts-kernel-cbd2b3acf-bootimg-066279cc), "host ports are weak sources"), less than the idle load, so the battery slowly discharges while `status` stays `Charging` (live 2026-10-24, screen off: `bms/current_now` +31 to +42 mA, `battery/current_now` the same, negative). powerd then shows Charging and UPower discharging, and both are right: UPower follows the current.

## UPower

`upowerd` (Alpine's `upower` 1.91.0, with `upower-udev`) puts the battery and the power supplies on the system bus as `org.freedesktop.UPower`, for `upower -d` and any client written for it. It runs as the supervised OpenRC service `upower` ([services](services.md)), as user `upower` with no capabilities ([users and permissions](users-and-permissions.md)); its GLib warnings go to syslog (`logread | grep upowerd`) and its history files to `/var/lib/upower` (the volatile `/var`, every 600 s). It reads the `battery` supply (the percentage from `capacity`, the state from `status` and the sign of `current_now`, the energy from the charge attributes above) and the line-power supplies `usb`, `pc_port`, `dc`, `usbeb` and `wireless` (`bms` and `main` have types it does not know and are skipped), on the kernel's uevents and every 30 s.

It only reports. `rootfs-overlay/etc/UPower/UPower.conf` (the whole file; `UPower.conf.d` stays empty) gives it powerd's levels (by percentage: low 15 %, critical 5 %, action 2 %), the critical action `Ignore` with `AllowRiskyCriticalPowerAction=true`, `IgnoreLid=true` and no Watts Up Pro. upowerd logs the same lines at every start, all expected and none an error: the "considered risky" warning (because of `Ignore`); "Could not acquire inhibitor lock" (no logind); once per supply type it does not know, "did not recognize type ..., please report" for `Wipower`, `Main`, `USB_PD`, `USB_DCP`, `Wireless` and `BMS`; and "USB power supply ... pc_port without usb_type property". powerd stays the one owner of the low-battery and shutdown policy; without logind upowerd could not power off anyway. It writes sysfs only on an `EnableChargeThreshold` call, which our battery cannot take (no `charge_control_*` attributes).

There is no D-Bus activation: the image build removes the package's `org.freedesktop.UPower.service`, which would start a root upowerd whenever the service is down, so while the `upower` service is stopped no client finds the name. Our bus policy `usr/share/dbus-1/system.d/chef-upower.conf` lets user `upower` own `org.freedesktop.UPower`; the package's policy (everyone may call it) stays as shipped.

Live 2026-10-24 (RAM image, kernel #24): upowerd ran as `upower` (207) with no capabilities; `upower -d` showed 82 %, energy 16.4 of 20.5 Wh, the voltage, charge-cycles 1, the temperature, a time-to-empty estimate and the five line-power supplies with `usb` online; `battery/current_now` was minus `bms/current_now` in every sample; its history files in `/var/lib/upower` were owned by `upower`, and it wrote nothing else. Guided on the installed pair the same day: unplugged `discharging` (battery -155 mA, about 9 h to empty), on the wall charger (`USB_DCP`) `charging` (+627 mA, 1.6 h to full), with `battery/current_now` the opposite of `bms/current_now` in both. An unplugged A/B with the screen off (60 s blocks, both services stopped against running: 112.0, 118.6, 113.0, 111.6 mA) showed no cost beyond its spread; both had 0 CPU ticks in 60 s idle.

## Inspect

In the phone shell:

```sh
powerd status                 # the state file
cat /data/v1/power/log.csv    # history across boots (/run/chef/power/log.csv on a RAM-only boot)
logread | grep powerd        # transitions: plug/unplug, status, warnings, throttle, shutdown
upower -d                     # UPower's view: the battery (percentage, state, energy, time) and the line-power supplies
cat /sys/class/power_supply/battery/current_now /sys/class/power_supply/bms/current_now   # opposite signs
```

State keys include `status`, `source` (`usb`, `dc`, `battery` or `unknown`), `usb_type` (`USB_CDP`, `USB_DCP`, `USB` for SDP, ...), `capacity`, `voltage_mv`, `current_ma`, `temp_c`, `charge_counter_uah`, `usb_input_ma`, `usb_voltage_mv`, `input_settled_ma`, `throttle_level`, `alert` (`none`, `low`, `critical`, `confirming`, `shutdown`) and `overrides` (empty in production).

`profile_fcc_ma` is `battery/constant_charge_current_max`, which is only the battery-profile FCC vote. The throttle's vote does not show there. The effective charge current limit is the programmed FCC register, read directly from `main/constant_charge_current_max` (3000000 at level 0, 1500000 on stock at level 3), and it is also in the kernel heartbeat line `EFFECTIVE: FV = …, FCC = …, USBICL = …` in dmesg. powerd does not log the `main` value yet.

## Test options

Each of these is logged as `TEST OVERRIDE` and listed in the state file's `overrides=`:
`--warn`, `--critical`, `--rearm`, `--empty-pct`, `--empty-mv`, `--overtemp`, `--throttle-set`, `--throttle-clear`, `--no-throttle`, `--confirm`, `--confirm-gap-ms`, `--step-ms`, `--poll-s`, `-x CMD` (run `sh -c CMD` instead of powering off, with no fallback) and `--shutdown-when-online` (run the low-battery policy as if unpowered, to test the shutdown with the USB control link attached). `powerd` refuses `critical > warn` or `rearm <= warn`.

## Modify and verify

`make -C tools/powerd test` runs `tools/powerd/tests/test_powerd.c` (policy, SDP/unknown handling, throttle, outputs and cadence against a fake sysfs tree with injected hooks; its `battery` and `bms` currents have opposite signs, so a powerd reading `battery/current_now` fails) and `tools/powerd/tests/test_powerd.sh` (the host binary end to end, shutting down through `-x` only). `scripts/tests/test_services.py` checks the `upower` service's command line, `UPower.conf`'s lines, the removed activation file and, on a private `dbus-daemon`, that `chef-upower.conf` lets its user own `org.freedesktop.UPower` and nothing else. The kernel side is `smb2_batt_get_prop()` in `kernel/drivers/power/supply/qcom/qpnp-smb2.c`. Then rebuild the image (the kernel too for a `qpnp-smb2.c` change).

## Live verification (2026-09-26, QPTS kernel `cbd2b3acf`, `boot.img` `066279cc…`)

See the [build-log entry](../build-log.md) and `logs/powerd-live-test-2026-09-26-*`.

- **The kernel charges on its own.** On a wall charger (`USB_DCP`, then `USB_PD` after negotiation, input settled at 2575 mA), `current_now` peaked at −1.98 A (before kernel #24; `bms/current_now` has that sign today), and `bms/charge_counter` rose by 302 mAh in 14.2 min (about 1.28 A mean, including 4 min throttled by the L3 test). Net from before the plug to after the unplug it rose 274 mAh, which matches 80 → 85 % of 5004 mAh. Unplugged, it fell at about +310 mA. The counter also steps at plug events (+5 mAh while still `Discharging` at plug-in, −26 mAh just before the unplug), so drain maths must never span a plug event. The profile, capacity and `charge_full` match stock.
- **Host ports are weak sources.** The PC's CDP port advertised 1500 mA but AICL settled the input at 125 to 175 mA, so the phone net-discharged while reporting `Charging`, exactly as on stock. Use a wall charger or power bank to actually charge.
- Plug/unplug transitions were logged within one uevent. The wall charger bounced once during detection (`Not charging` → `Discharging` for about 1.2 s); the 3-sample confirmation ignores that.
- The throttle stepped `system_temp_level` 0 → 7 with a test set point. The effective FCC (heartbeat) went to 300 mA, the charge current fell from 1.6 A to 0.27 A, and the level reset to 0 on unplug. Only the step-up was exercised live.
- The low-battery shutdown with test thresholds went LOW → CRITICAL (buzz) → 3 confirmations → SHUTDOWN in 11 s, then power-off into Android's off-mode charger. The evidence for the power-off is the host kmsg stream closing right after the `SHUTDOWN` line plus the user seeing the charger screen; no init shutdown or power-down line was captured.
- **Heat:** on the wall charger the battery went from 32 °C to 42 °C in about 7 min at room temperature, so the 44 °C throttle will engage in real use (sun, handlebars, power bank).
- **Open observation, not parity:** stock was read at `system_temp_level=3` (`main/constant_charge_current_max` 1500000) with the battery at 37 °C, 30 s after boot (`logs/powerd-live-test-2026-09-26-stock-before-after.txt`). Stock can therefore throttle charging below 44 °C, probably through the `SS-CHG-BATT` rule on `msm_therm` or as a boot transient (one sample). powerd's battery-only 44 °C rule is less conservative than stock.
- Not yet seen live: `Full` and the recharge restart, a real 68 °C shutdown, a real throttle at 44 °C, an SDP port, and a real empty-battery shutdown or full-ride drain.

## Limits

- Current state and shutdown markers live in `/run` (RAM). The power log survives a clean shutdown on the owned writable `/data` layout; RAM-only boots still lose it.
- With the installed standalone layout, poweroff with USB attached returns to our image in charger mode; unplugging before poweroff is needed to stay off. Our image never writes persist.
- Cycle count and battery age are not persisted by a consumer yet. The fuel gauge keeps the cycle count while the battery stays connected.

Remaining validation and persistence work is owned by [power and reliability](../next-steps/power-and-reliability.md#battery-and-charging).
